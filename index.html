Feasibility Study: Small-Scale Plastic Pyrolysis System

Project Overview

This project explores the technical and economic feasibility of a small-scale plastic pyrolysis system to convert plastic waste into usable liquid fuel through thermal decomposition in an oxygen-free environment.

Project Goal

To design and evaluate a cost-effective and sustainable small-scale pyrolysis system suitable for plastic waste management and energy recovery applications in Sri Lanka.

Objectives

  • Evaluate pyrolysis technology for plastic waste conversion.
  • Design a small-scale batch pyrolysis reactor system.
  • Analyze technical performance and fuel yield.
  • Conduct cost analysis and economic evaluation.
  • Assess environmental benefits and sustainability.

Key Benefits

  • Reduces plastic pollution and landfill dependency.
  • Converts waste plastic into valuable liquid fuel.
  • Supports sustainable waste-to-energy solutions.
  • Provides potential for small-scale industrial applications.

Technologies Used

  • Pyrolysis process analysis
  • Heat transfer and mass transfer analysis
  • Material and energy balance
  • CAD modelling and system layout design
  • Cost estimation and economic analysis

Project Highlights

  • Designed a 30 L batch pyrolysis reactor system.
  • Developed reactor, condenser and system layout concepts.
  • Estimated fuel yield and system performance.
  • Performed cost-benefit and payback analysis.
  • Prepared CAD models and technical documentation.

Methodology

The study followed a research-based methodology including literature review, plastic type selection, process temperature analysis, reactor concept design, heat exchanger design, fuel yield estimation and economic feasibility evaluation.

Results & Outcome

The analysis showed that small-scale plastic pyrolysis has strong potential as a waste-to-energy solution. The proposed system demonstrated possible fuel recovery, environmental benefit and practical application for local waste management.
